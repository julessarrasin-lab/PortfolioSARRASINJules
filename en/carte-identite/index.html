In detail

My qualities. I recognise myself in several qualities: curiosity, perseverance and a taste for work. I like having a goal to reach and looking for a solution when a problem comes up. Sport and my work experience in restaurants have also taught me to keep going despite tiredness and difficulties.

Pressure. For a long time I had difficulty with high-pressure situations, especially in the restaurant when an unusual number of customers arrived at the same time. I sometimes let myself be overwhelmed emotionally. With experience, I understood that I could step back, put the situation into perspective and keep acting despite the pressure.

My working method. At the start of my studies, my working method was almost non-existent. I gradually understood that waiting until the last moment did not let me make full use of my abilities. So I learned to start my work earlier.

The turning point. A teacher once wrote on my test: “You can do better, much better, you have potential.” That remark struck me. It gave me a push in my work and truly motivated me.

Working in a group. My experience in restaurants and in sport has taught me that a group works when everyone accepts doing their part and keeps going despite difficulties.

Today. When I face a difficulty, I try more to step back, analyse what is happening and look for a solution rather than put up with the situation.
