My background

From a handball sports programme to the IUT of Aix-en-Provence: a path built around science, sport and effort.

Middle school

Handball, then STI2D

A young person from the Alps, passionate about science and sport, I joined a sports programme specialising in handball as early as middle school. I learned team spirit there, and how to accept failure.

After that, I chose the STI2D stream (Science and Technology of Industry and Sustainable Development).

High school

Boarding school and triathlon

I joined the boarding school of the Pierre-Gilles de Gennes high school. During that time, I developed real independence in my work, being alone in my boarding-school room.

Alongside that, I continued my sporting path towards triathlon, a sport that attracted me for its rigour and resilience. It gave me a desire to push my limits and to be better every day.

June 2025

Baccalauréat with honours

I finished my final year by earning my baccalauréat with honours (mention Bien), and a place at the university of my choice: the IUT of Aix-en-Provence.

Today

BUT 2 Mechanical and Production Engineering

I am now in my second year of the BUT, where I learn to develop and size engineering projects.
