About me

My name is Jules Sarrasin and I am in my second year of the BUT in Mechanical and Production Engineering. Curious, dynamic and determined, I want to keep improving and always do better.

Mechanics and design let me put what I learn in class into practice and turn it into concrete solutions. I like to understand how things work.

Sport plays an important part in my life: running, skiing, hiking, triathlon, handball… including a 250 km trek between Gap and Nice. Mechanics is also one of my interests, through repairing cars and farm vehicles and designing 3D objects.

These experiences have helped me develop rigour, perseverance, curiosity and independence, and have strengthened my ability to solve problems.
